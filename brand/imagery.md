# Imagery

Style lock for generated photos. `tools/imagine.mjs` prepends the block under "Prompt" to every request unless `--raw` is passed.

Images carry people, places and light only. Text, logos and app UI never come from the image model: they are real layers and real screens in the post.

## Prompt

Editorial lifestyle photograph, Brazilian people, natural and candid, genuine relaxed expressions. Soft warm natural window light, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35-50mm lens. Lived-in Brazilian homes and clinics with plants, light wood and linen. Subtle lilac and soft violet accents in clothing or decor, never neon, never green as a dominant colour. Any phone visible has a completely blank pure white screen with no UI, no text, no reflections, front-facing and unobstructed by fingers. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks anywhere in the image. Photorealistic, no illustration, no 3D render look.

## Never

- Text, captions, logos, brand marks, app UI
- Green-dominant palettes, neon, heavy filters, HDR look
- Stock-photo poses, thumbs up, pointing at camera
- Phones at extreme angles or with fingers covering the screen
