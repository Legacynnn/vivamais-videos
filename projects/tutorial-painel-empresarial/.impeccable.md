## Design Context

Sources: `BRIEF.md`, `frame.md`, and the owner's review notes of 2026-09-29.

### Users
RH e gestores de empresas que contratam ou já usam o plano empresarial Viva Mais. They watch to learn the panel end to end: register the company, add vidas, set credits, pay, schedule, follow usage. They are not designers and not technical; they need to see where to click.

### Brand Personality
Warm, clinical, light. Energetic, youthful, human. Calm enough for healthcare; never cold, bureaucratic, fintech-loud, neon, or generic SaaS.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Ice-white canvas `#FAFAFE`, ink type `#17151E`, solid violet `#7E3BED` / `#6A24DB`, mist `#F3EDFF`, rare lime `#C6FF34`.
- DM Sans for all video type. The app UI keeps Poppins.
- App UI inside the device is the real captured DOM with a light polish layer (`build/polish.css`): one violet instead of four, violet-tinted neutrals, tonal card edges instead of heavy shadows, slightly larger radii, tighter headings, tabular numbers. Layout and content stay as the app ships them.
- Logo fixed top-left. No label rows, no stepper lists, no progress rails.
- No gradient text, no side-stripe accents, no bounce easing.

### Design Principles
1. Show the real product; polish it, do not redraw it.
2. Every pointer, tap, drag and highlight lands exactly on its target (`npm run audit`).
3. Text comes to the action: callouts attach to the element being explained and move with it.
4. Big type carries the chapter changes; the device carries the detail.
5. Payment moments are acted out start to finish, not implied.
