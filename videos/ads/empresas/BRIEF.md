# Viva Mais para empresas

Ad: motion graphics, silent, 45.0s. B2B: sells the Viva Mais company plan to HR and owners. Revision 2 (owner feedback `FEEDBACK-2026-11-16.md`; owner answers 2026-11-16 are binding and folded in below).

## Message

Sua empresa dá saúde de verdade ao time, tudo em um aplicativo: escolhe o plano, cadastra as vidas, e o time consulta quando quiser, sem carência, com dependentes ilimitados; a empresa ganha retenção, menos faltas e gasta até 10x menos que um plano de saúde. Peça uma proposta.

## Audience

Company owners, HR and managers, 35–60 (type sized for 50+). Instagram/Facebook Reels and Stories, LinkedIn, WhatsApp. Muted autoplay: every beat is carried by on-screen text.

## Look: floating motion UI on the Viva Mais light stage

Same family as `ads/consulta-especialistas` (spawn/morph grammar, `.hk-grad` sheen, marquee rows) and `ads/plantao-24h-v2` (tap dot, eases), mostly motion graphics: UI pieces float on the stage; the patient phone appears in one scene only (`time`); the company panel never appears as a screen.

- **Shared styles**: `assets/em.css` (prefix `em-`). Link it in every composition after `assets/brand/tokens.css`. Every colour is a `--vm-*` or `--em-*` var; no hex literals, no raw `rgba()`. `em.css` is shared: builders do not edit it; scene-only rules go in the scene `<style>` under `#<id>`. Three shared components were added for this round (use them, do not re-implement):
  - **Gradient accent** `.em-line em` and `.em-grad-text`: violet-700 → 600 → 500 → **300 sheen** → 500 → 600 → 700, `background-size 260%`, resting at `background-position 100% 0%`. **Sheen pass**: one tween per accent, `backgroundPosition "100% 0%" → "0% 0%"`, 1.4s, `power2.inOut`, starting when the accent word lands (exactly as `.hk-grad` in `consulta-especialistas/compositions/hook.html`). Once per accent appearance, never looped or yoyo'd. Every `<em>` in the film uses it (owner: "nossos degradês em todo o texto colorido").
  - **Check** `.em-check` (84px violet-100 tile, radius 32%) containing `<svg viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path pathLength="1" d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"/></svg>`. **Draw-on**: on the land frame add `.em-check--on` (tile fills violet, stroke white), tween the path `strokeDashoffset 1 → 0` 0.3s `power2.out`, and pop the tile `scale 1 → 1.12 → 1` (0.12s `power2.out` + 0.2s `power3.out`). Used in `regras`, `empresa`, `fim`.
  - **Marquee** `.em-marquee` (full-width row, edge mask 120px each side) > `.em-marquee-strip` (flex, gap 28). Position is a pure function of absolute time: `x = x0 ∓ v·(S + t)` set with one linear tween from scene start to end (`ease: "none"`), constant velocity, never eased, never looped by reset inside the scene; repeat the items enough that the strip never shows its end. Used in `hero` (chips) and `provas` (quotes).
- **Stage**: every scene root is `<div class="vm-scene em-scene">` with `.em-stage` (canvas → mist) and `.em-aurora` (`.a1 .a2 .a3`). Aurora drift is one continuous global move: each blob's `x = -7 * T` px and `y = -3 * T` px where `T` is absolute video time; a scene starting at `S` with duration `D` tweens linearly from `T = S` to `T = S + D`. `fim` uses `.em-stage--white` with no aurora.
- **Colour**: violet dominant. **Zero green**: no `--vm-spark-*`, `--vm-secondary*`, `--vm-success*`, emerald or teal anywhere (live dots, checks, toggles, selected states are violet). Only exception: the AI orb's aqua blob. No cross-hue gradients; gradients allowed: the same-hue accent text above and `--em-grad` on the final button. Library screens with spark inline styles are recoloured by the `.vms [style*="18, 255, 215"]` rules in `em.css`; check snapshots for any other green.
- **Type**: lines `.em-line` 84px Space Grotesk 500, ink, one gradient `<em>`, top y 330, left 80, max 2 lines (exceptions sized in the table: hero headline 92px, `time` lines 68px one line). Body/readable copy ≥ 52px DM Sans unless the table says otherwise (deviations listed). Big numbers Space Grotesk 400/500, tabular. No eyebrows.
- **Chips** (`.em-chip`): landing HeroBenefits chip scaled ×3.5 (132px tall, white 92%, `.em-badge` + title 52px DM Sans 500). Title only.
- **Icons**: Lucide only, from `node_modules/lucide-static/icons/<name>.svg` inlined, stroke 1.9, `currentColor`, inside `.em-tile`, `.em-badge` or a plano icon chip. No custom sprites, no hugeicons.
- **Phone**: kit iPhone (`.cam > .dev > .bezel + .screen > .scr`) upright and flat. In `time` only: `.cam` left 263, top 600, width 554, height 1167; `.dev` scale 1.3 (screen rect left 279, top 616, 523×1136, radius 70). Screen changes are `k.push` (left), never crossfades.
- **Motion grammar** (`motion-doctrine` + `cut-the-curve`, house overrides in bold):
  - One current: the film moves LEFT. Every non-morph seam is cut-the-curve LEFT: exit `x 0 → −230` `power4.in` 0.34s with opacity done by ~30% of travel, entry `x +230 → 0` `power4.out` 0.44s igniting at opacity 0.35, cut mid-motion on both sides. **Blur never above 4px** (house rule beats the skill's 8–10px).
  - Lists arrive as **waterfall entries** (§6): from below (or from the right inside a LEFT seam), overlapping, gaps shrinking ×0.84, heavy items travel 60–80px, light 30–48px, `power4.out`, opacity binary via `tl.set`. **Total stagger ≤ 0.5s** per list.
  - Group repositions use the **nudge curve** (§7): `power3.in` 10% distance/20% time → `none` 65%/18% → `power4.out` 25%/62%.
  - Text swaps in place: planSwitch `swapText` (old leaves y −40% + blur 4, new rises from +40%, 0.24s, `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.1,0.25,1)`); in `time` 0.2s. Numbers roll like the site's `RollingNumber` (per-digit vertical tracks, `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`).
  - No idle wobble, no looping glow. Loops allowed only as constant-velocity marquees (hero chips, provas quotes) and the final button pulse (owner asked).
- **Tap**: `.em-tap` + `.em-tap-ring` (glide 0.45s power3.inOut, press scale 0.86 for 0.1s, ring 1→1.8 once, target changes on the press frame).
- **Reading pace**: every line or text group fully legible (opacity 1, blur 0) for **≥ 1.5s**. One `.em-line` at a time.
- **Logo**: `assets/brand/lockup-cor.svg` as `.em-logo` (x 80, y 220, w 210) only in `hero` (from 0.6) and `proposta` (from 0.15).
